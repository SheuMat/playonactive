Saturday Club

Kick off the weekend the active way.

A weekend multi-sport club for primary-age children. A great fit if weekday clubs don't work for your family, or your child simply can't get enough sport.

Age groups

Pitched at the right level

Children play with others at a similar stage, so the youngest build confidence and the oldest get a proper challenge. Choose a group to see what they'll focus on.

Reception & Year 1

Playful, story-led games that build the basics of movement and help little ones feel confident in a group.

Focus areas

  • Running, hopping and jumping
  • Balance and coordination games
  • Listening and taking turns

Years 2 & 3

Core sport skills taught through fun challenges and small-sided games.

Focus areas

  • Throwing, catching and kicking
  • Dodging and finding space
  • Playing as part of a team

Years 4 to 6

Sport-specific skills, tactics and friendly competition, which is great preparation for secondary school PE and local teams.

Focus areas

  • Technique in specific sports
  • Tactics and decision-making
  • Leadership and resilience
