Saturday Club

A Saturday full of choice and discovery

Our Saturday Club provides a fun, welcoming environment where children can explore a variety of activities, make new friends and enjoy being active.

From sports and movement challenges to creative activities and social games, children have the freedom to discover what they enjoy in a supportive, play-based environment.

What can children enjoy?

Something for every child

Sport & Movement

A variety of sports, movement challenges, active games and opportunities to develop new skills.

Creative Activities

Drawing, crafts, imaginative activities and collaborative projects that encourage creativity and self-expression.

Games & Discovery

Social games, puzzles, group challenges and exciting activities that encourage teamwork, problem-solving and friendships.

How it works

How Saturday Club works

Our Saturday Club combines independent exploration with organised group activities.

Children can move between supervised activity areas, choosing what interests them while having opportunities to participate in games and challenges with others.

Our approach encourages children to develop confidence, independence and physical literacy through play.
