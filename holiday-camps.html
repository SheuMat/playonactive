Holiday Camps

PlayOn Active Holiday Camps

Play. Discover. Create.

Fun, welcoming holiday camps for children in School Years 1–5 (ages 6–10).

Register your interest A day at camp

Our holiday camps are packed with exciting activities, creative experiences and opportunities for children to explore, play and make new friends.

From energetic games and team challenges to creative activities and discovering new sports, every day offers something different.

Designed for children in School Years 1–5 (ages 6–10), our camps provide a fun, welcoming and inclusive environment where everyone can get involved.

What happens at camp?

Every day offers something different

Get Moving

Exciting movement games, active challenges and activities that develop coordination, balance and agility.

Discover Sports

Opportunities to experience different sports, learn new skills and explore new interests in a fun, pressure-free environment.

Team Challenges

Group games and collaborative activities that encourage teamwork, communication and problem-solving.

Get Creative

Arts, crafts and imaginative activities that encourage children to express themselves and explore their creativity.

Play & Explore

A variety of engaging games and activities where children can make friends, build confidence and, most importantly, have fun.
