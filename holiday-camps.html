Themed weeks

A fresh theme every holiday

Each camp has a theme to keep children engaged, with structured sport, team games and age-appropriate challenges throughout the day.

Olympics Week

Athletics, team events and a medal ceremony — children compete for their nation across the week.

World Cup Week

Football skills, mini-tournaments and team spirit — every child gets a shirt and a squad.

Ninja Warriors Week

Obstacle courses, agility challenges and team missions that build strength, coordination and confidence.

Themes rotate each holiday — get in touch or join the mailing list for upcoming dates.

A day at camp

Busy, active and well supervised

  • Early drop-off — get to work on time with optional wraparound from the morning.
  • Morning sport — themed multi-activity sessions and skill-building challenges.
  • Lunch & downtime — refuel and recharge with the group.
  • Afternoon games — team challenges, tournaments and the big finish.
  • Late pick-up — relaxed wind-down until you arrive.

Children should bring a packed lunch, water bottle, weather-appropriate clothing and trainers.
