About PlayOn Active

We help children fall in love with moving.

Our mission is simple: to develop confident, competent and motivated movers through high-quality physical literacy sessions for primary-age children.

What we believe

Enjoyment first. Progress follows.

Children keep doing what they enjoy. So we start with fun, energy and making sure every child feels part of the group, and build real skills from there.

Whether it's an after-school club, a holiday camp or a Saturday session, every PlayOn Active session has a clear purpose: to help children feel good about being active, now and for life.

Physical literacy

The four things we build in every child

Physical literacy is the mix of confidence, skill, motivation and understanding that keeps people active for life. Tap each one to see how it shows up.

{{icon:star}}

Confidence

The self-belief to take part, have a go and not worry about getting it wrong.

What you might notice

  • Joining in without holding back
  • Trying a new sport without fuss
  • Talking proudly about what they did
{{icon:target}}

Competence

The movement skills to take part successfully, built step by step.

What you might notice

  • Better balance and coordination
  • Catching, throwing and kicking with more control
  • Keeping up in the playground and in PE
{{icon:bolt}}

Motivation

Genuinely wanting to be active, again and again.

What you might notice

  • Asking when the next session is
  • Playing the games at home
  • Wanting to try a new club or team
{{icon:sparkle}}

Knowledge

Understanding why moving matters and how to stay healthy and active.

What you might notice

  • Knowing why we warm up and cool down
  • Talking about how exercise makes them feel
  • Making active choices on their own

The building blocks

Mastering the ABCs

Underneath every sport are the same three skills. We weave them into every session, so whatever your child plays next comes more easily.

  • A

    Agility

    Changing direction and speed with control.

  • B

    Balance

    Staying stable, on the move and standing still.

  • C

    Coordination

    Moving different parts of the body together smoothly.
