Sports we cover

One club. Loads of sports.

We rotate activities so children try a bit of everything and find the one that clicks. Tap a sport to see what they'll work on.

{{icon:ball}}

Football

Small-sided games that get every child on the ball, not just the confident few.

What they'll practise

  • Dribbling and close control
  • Passing to a teammate
  • Finding space and teamwork
{{icon:bolt}}

Athletics

Running, jumping and throwing challenges where children race their own best as well as each other.

What they'll practise

  • Sprinting and relay changeovers
  • Jumping for height and distance
  • Throwing technique
{{icon:star}}

Gymnastics

Safe, mat-based movement that builds strength, flexibility and body control.

What they'll practise

  • Balances and shapes
  • Rolls and safe landings
  • Linking moves into a sequence
{{icon:target}}

Invasion games

Tag rugby, basketball, handball and more: games about getting the ball into the other team's space.

What they'll practise

  • Attacking and defending
  • Quick decisions under pressure
  • Communicating as a team
{{icon:sun}}

Striking & fielding

Cricket and rounders-style games with plenty of hitting, catching and running between bases.

What they'll practise

  • Hand-eye coordination
  • Catching and throwing accurately
  • Taking turns and fair play
{{icon:sparkle}}

Net & wall games

Short tennis, badminton and volleyball-style games, adapted so even first-timers get a rally going.

What they'll practise

  • Racket and ball control
  • Footwork and positioning
  • Keeping score and playing fair
{{icon:smile}}

Dodgeball

A firm favourite. Soft balls, simple rules and non-stop movement.

What they'll practise

  • Agility and quick reactions
  • Throwing and catching
  • Team tactics
{{icon:medal}}

Multi-skills

Fun circuits and games built around the fundamentals every sport needs.

What they'll practise

  • Agility, balance and coordination
  • Running, jumping and landing
  • Confidence to try new things
