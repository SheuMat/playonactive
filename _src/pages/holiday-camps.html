PlayOn Active Holiday Camps

Play. Discover. Create.

Fun, welcoming holiday camps for children in School Years 1–5 (ages 6–10).

Our holiday camps are packed with exciting activities, creative experiences and opportunities for children to explore, play and make new friends.

From energetic games and team challenges to creative activities and discovering new sports, every day offers something different.

Designed for children in School Years 1–5 (ages 6–10), our camps provide a fun, welcoming and inclusive environment where everyone can get involved.

What happens at camp?
