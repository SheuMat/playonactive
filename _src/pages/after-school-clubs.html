Inside a session

What happens at club

Every session follows a simple rhythm, so children know what to expect and get the most out of their time. Tap each part to see more.

Part 1

Warm-up games

We start with quick, active games that raise the heart rate and get everyone involved from the first whistle. They're a great way to shake off a long day at a desk.

{{icon:bolt}} Energy{{icon:smile}} Settling in{{icon:users}} Joining in

Part 2

Skill time

The coach introduces the skill of the day, such as passing, balancing or throwing, and breaks it into simple steps. Children practise in small groups with plenty of encouragement and tips.

{{icon:target}} Technique{{icon:star}} Confidence{{icon:chart}} Progress

Part 3

Game on

Then it's time to use the new skill in a real game. Small-sided games mean lots of touches for everyone, and plenty of chances to make decisions and work as a team.

{{icon:users}} Teamwork{{icon:bolt}} Decision-making{{icon:heart}} Fair play

Part 4

Cool-down & shout-outs

We finish calmly with a stretch and a quick chat about what went well. Children are recognised for effort, kindness and improvement, not just goals scored.

{{icon:medal}} Recognition{{icon:heart}} Wellbeing{{icon:sparkle}} Reflection
