About PlayOn Active

Developing physical literacy through play.

Our mission is to help children become confident, competent and motivated movers, and to make being active something they genuinely love.

What we believe

Play first. Progress follows.

Children keep doing what they enjoy. So everything we run starts with play: games, movement, creativity and the freedom to try new things with friends.

Through play, children build the skills, confidence and curiosity that help them stay active for life, whether that's at Saturday Club, a holiday camp or an after-school club.

Physical literacy

Four things we help every child build

Physical literacy is the mix of confidence, skill, motivation and understanding that keeps people active for life. Tap each one to see how it shows up.

Confidence

The self-belief to join in, have a go and not worry about getting it wrong.

What you might notice

  • Joining in without holding back
  • Trying something new without fuss
  • Talking proudly about their day

Competence

Movement skills that grow through games and play.

What you might notice

  • Better balance and coordination
  • More control when running, jumping and catching
  • Keeping up happily in the playground

Motivation

Genuinely wanting to be active, again and again.

What you might notice

  • Asking when the next session is
  • Playing the games at home
  • Wanting to try new activities

Knowledge

Understanding why moving matters and how it makes us feel.

What you might notice

  • Talking about how being active makes them feel
  • Making active choices on their own
  • Inventing their own games

The building blocks

Agility, balance & coordination

These three skills sit underneath every game and sport. We weave them into play, so everything children try next comes more easily.

  • A

    Agility

    Changing direction and speed with control.

  • B

    Balance

    Staying steady, on the move and standing still.

  • C

    Coordination

    Moving different parts of the body together smoothly.
