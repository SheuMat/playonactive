Clubs built on physical literacy

Every club follows our physical literacy approach, so children get the enjoyment of sport alongside genuine development in movement skills, confidence and teamwork.

  • Fundamental movement skills — agility, balance and coordination woven through every session.
  • Confidence & participation — a positive, encouraging environment for every child.
  • Qualified, DBS-checked coaches — safe, reliable and positive with children.
  • Consistent faces — children build a relationship with their coach across the block.

How clubs are funded

Flexible, affordable and easy to run

Clubs can be paid for directly by parents or funded by the school through PE & Sport Premium, whichever works best for your community.

Parents book their child onto the termly block directly. Simple online sign-up, sibling-friendly.
