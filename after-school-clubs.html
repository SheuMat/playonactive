After-School Clubs

PlayOn Active After-School Clubs

Developing Physical Literacy Through Play

Enquire about a club What to expect

Our after-school clubs help children develop physical literacy through fun, engaging and inclusive play-based activities.

Through a variety of games, movement challenges and opportunities to explore different sports, children are encouraged to discover new skills, build confidence and enjoy being active.

Our sessions focus on developing fundamental movement skills, coordination and balance, while encouraging creativity, teamwork and a lifelong love of physical activity.

What to expect

Movement & Coordination

Games & Challenges

Introducing children to a variety of sports in a fun, pressure-free environment.

Confidence & Social Skills

Building friendships, independence and self-belief through play.

Fun & Inclusion

Creating a welcoming environment where every child can participate and thrive.
