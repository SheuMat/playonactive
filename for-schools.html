What we offer

Flexible delivery, tailored to your timetable

Book us for a single day a week or build a wider PE partnership across the term. We align to your timetable, year groups, priorities and reporting needs.

  • PE curriculum delivery — progressive, mapped schemes of work for R–Y6.
  • PPA cover — qualified specialist coaches releasing your teachers.
  • Assessment & progress tracking — termly reports for your subject leads.
  • After-school clubs — extend the offer beyond the school day.
  • Holiday camps — signposted provision for your families.
  • Staff CPD — building capacity within your own team.
